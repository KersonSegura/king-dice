FULL DESCRIPTION
----------------
King Dice is your board game community in your pocket. Connect with players, share your table, and discover what others are playing—all in one app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Community Feed – See posts, setups, and photos from other players. Share your own games and collections.

• Gallery – Browse and upload photos of game setups, collections, and events. Like and comment on others’ posts.

• Chat – Talk with the community and stay in the loop.

• Game Collection – Build and view your collection and discover hot and top-ranked board games.

• Virtual Tools – Roll dice, flip coins, and run timers in one place.

• Game Night Tracker – Log sessions and track wins if you like to keep stats.

• More tools – Links to the full King Dice experience (Catan maps, Boardle, Pixel Canvas, and more) so you can go from phone to desktop without missing a beat.

Join the King Dice community and share your table.
